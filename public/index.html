Questions

What do I need?

Just your Phishnet username. If you don't have one, create a free account at phish.net and add your shows. The more complete your show history, the better your document.

How is this different from just looking at my Phishnet profile?

Phishnet gives you data. We turn that data into a narrative — era analysis, personality reads, crown jewel deep dives, and a letter to your past self.

Is this a good gift?

It will be. Gift purchases will be available when the full report launches. Join the waitlist to be notified.

How long does delivery take?

The free Snapshot is instant. The full Phish Story report (coming soon) will be delivered within 48 hours of purchase.

What if I only have a few shows?

Even 5 shows make a great document. The narrative adapts to your history — whether you're a first-timer or a 500-show vet.
