Questions

What do I need?

Just your Phishnet username. If you don't have one, create a free account at phish.net and add your shows. The more complete your show history, the better your document.

How is this different from just looking at my Phishnet profile?

Phishnet gives you data. We turn that data into a narrative — era analysis, personality reads, crown jewel deep dives, a letter to your past self. The stats are free. The story isn't.

Is this a good gift?

It's the best gift. "Your friend just hit their 100th show" is basically the reason this exists. Use the gift option at checkout — we'll deliver directly to their inbox.

How long does delivery take?

48 hours or less. Each document is individually generated, not templated. We're in early access and manually reviewing every order.

What if I only have a few shows?

Even 5 shows make a great document. The narrative adapts to your history — whether you're a first-timer or a 500-show vet.
