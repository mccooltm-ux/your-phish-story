## Distribution Sequencing

1. **Personal network first** (5-10 friends) — get first 3-5 orders + feedback
2. **r/phish post** — highest density of target customers
3. **Twitter/X thread** — broader reach, shareable
4. **Phantasy Tour** — engaged community, longer discussions

## Key Principles

- **Lead with the free snapshot** — it's the hook. Every post should make it easy to try for free.
- **Don't be salesy** — you're a fan who built something, not a marketer.
- **Show, don't tell** — link to the sample report, mention the free snapshot.
- **Gift angle** — "great gift for the phan who just hit 100 shows" is a strong secondary hook.
